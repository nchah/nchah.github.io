0. GraphIt Explorer

GraphIt was designed as a tool to help teach newcomers about concepts from the Linked Data and Semantic Web field. The tool creates a graph visualization of arbitrary triples that you can create for yourself.

The visualization is intended to make Linked Data fun and interactive, and also show the potential of Linked Data when it can be linked to other data sets (like those in the Linked Open Data Cloud).



1. A short introduction to the "triple"

A "SPO" triple is a line of data that always has three parts: (subject, predicate, object). A triple expresses information about a certain relationship (the predicate) between two entities (the subject and object).

This is similar to the structure of a simple sentence. The statement "Canada has a national capital: Ottawa." can be represented as a single triple: (Canada, capital, Ottawa).

Here are some triples you can paste into the box below to graph it:

Canada; capital; Ottawa
Canada; type; Country
Canada; officialLanguage; French_language
Canada; officialLanguage; English_language

The resulting graph is constructed for "Canada", with links to other related entities.



2. What kinds of entities and relationships are possible?

Almost any real world thing, event, location, or concept can be represented as an entity. And almost anything that relates two entities together can be considered as a relationship.

The formal definition of entities and relationships are often defined in something called the data set's ontology. This is a very simple explanation for a concept that is very deep in details (more information at this page, this page, and this page).
